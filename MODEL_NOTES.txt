PlantCare AI model notes

Primary model: BioCLIP 2 (open-source biology vision-language model)
Fallback model: Original BioCLIP
Classification approach: prompt ensembles + multi-view image ranking over 56 supported plant records.
Non-plant safeguards: general ImageNet object classifier when its weights load, plus a text/document screen. The app defers uncertain plant-vs-object cases to species matching rather than rejecting a plant on uncertain evidence.

Important limits
- Model weights are downloaded at runtime; first use needs internet access.
- BioCLIP 2 weights are large; hosting storage/memory can be limiting.
- The 56-class list limits output to those classes and does not cover every plant species.
- Similar species, poor lighting, distant subjects, mixed images and unusual growth stages may be misidentified.
- Displayed match scores are not calibrated probabilities.
- Plant Health Copilot gives symptom and care guidance, not laboratory disease diagnosis.
- Do not describe this app as 100% accurate or fully offline.
