PlantCare AI V13 Final Consolidated — model notes

Model sources used by the app:
- Imageomics BioCLIP: https://huggingface.co/imageomics/bioclip
- OpenCLIP: https://pypi.org/project/open-clip-torch/
- Streamlit: https://docs.streamlit.io/
- PyTorch: https://pytorch.org/get-started/locally/

BioCLIP is used through the OpenCLIP interface documented by Imageomics. The
model is loaded lazily and cached. The app uses BioCLIP for zero-shot plant
species matching and a separate prompt ensemble for strong non-plant rejection.
The species result is never treated as a calibrated probability.

Health/care analysis is intentionally conservative. Visible color/texture
patterns are screening signals only; a single RGB photograph cannot directly
measure soil moisture, fertilizer concentration, growth rate, light exposure,
or confirm a disease/pathogen. The UI explicitly separates visual screening
from species-specific care guidance.


Competition update notes
- The class prompt ensemble now includes species-specific hints for all 56 plant records, including more explicit contrastive descriptions for Tulsi (Ocimum tenuiflorum) and Mint (Mentha spicata).
- Multi-view ranking combines average view scores with a modest best-view contribution. This is a ranking heuristic, not a calibration method and not proof of improved accuracy without labelled validation.
- Plant Health Copilot is a transparent rule-based checklist implemented in plantcare_core.py. It does not use a disease classifier and must not be described as diagnosing plant disease.
- The ZIP does not include trained model weights or a labelled image evaluation set. BioCLIP and the general-object model download/load at runtime.
