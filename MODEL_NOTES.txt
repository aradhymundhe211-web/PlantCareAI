PlantCare AI — model and evidence notes

Species library: 56 plant records in plant_data.json.
Identification model: original BioCLIP only (chosen to reduce memory pressure on Streamlit Community Cloud).
Inference: open_clip image/text embeddings with botanical prompt descriptions and multiple image views. This is zero-shot ranking, not a custom-trained 56-class classifier.
Non-plant safeguards: ImageNet MobileNetV3-Small object screen (when weights are available), BioCLIP plant/object comparisons, and a document-like-image screen.
Camera/gallery parity: both inputs are decoded, EXIF-corrected and sent through the same image analysis functions.
No paid AI inference API is used by the identification or Copilot features. Model weights are downloaded at runtime, so initial startup needs network access and sufficient memory/storage. Reference photos in the library may also be fetched from public iNaturalist/Wikipedia endpoints.

Important limits
- The 56 names are supported candidate classes, not proof that every class can be identified accurately.
- No project-specific labelled dataset or independent 56-class benchmark is included.
- Displayed model ranking is not a calibrated probability.
- A model-loading failure now produces an unavailable result rather than a species guess from colour/manual clues.
- Visual health screening is a coarse colour-pattern screen, not leaf segmentation, disease recognition, pest detection, or measurement of soil moisture/nutrients.
- Plant Health Copilot is a local profile-retrieval and rule-based assistant, not a general-purpose language model or laboratory diagnosis.
- Never claim 100% accuracy. Validate using held-out photos from different devices, backgrounds, plant ages and lighting, plus non-plants and unsupported plants.
